# Engineering Scope & Assurance

The portfolio demonstrates selected software practices. It does not claim product certification, complete standards compliance or physical hardware qualification.

## Demonstrated practices

- API contracts, boundary conditions and deterministic fault injection.
- Static-analysis findings, behavioural tests and scoped coverage evidence.
- Isolated execution, pinned dependencies, source identity and artifact integrity checks.
- Explicit distinction between recorded execution, live local execution and deterministic analysis.

## Standards: relevance is not compliance

| Topic | Existing evidence | Not established by this portfolio |
| --- | --- | --- |
| MISRA | General C static-analysis and coding-review evidence | A complete rule/directive assessment, deviation process or MISRA compliance claim |
| Functional safety | Traceable assertions, fault injection and verified recovery | Hazard analysis, assigned integrity levels, a safety case, tool qualification or certification |
| Cybersecurity | Input allowlists, isolated execution and integrity checks | A complete threat/risk analysis, penetration assessment or secure-product lifecycle conformance |
| Medical-device software | Transferable software verification and traceability practices | IEC 62304 compliance, medical risk management or clinical validation |

Relevant standards must be chosen for the target product and role; they are not interchangeable. Standards knowledge or work experience should be stated only at its evidenced level.

## Validation boundary

No physical ECU, electrical CAN, MCU timing, hardware-in-the-loop qualification, production safety claim or medical/robotic deployment is established by these demonstrations. Hashes detect changed artifacts relative to their recorded reference; they are not a signed trust chain by themselves.
